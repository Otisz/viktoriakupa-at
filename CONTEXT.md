# Viktória Kupa

Public website of the Viktória Kupa acrobatic gymnastics competition (viktoriakupa.hu): news, club directory, regulation documents and competition info. Hungarian only.

## Language

### Content

**Post**:
A dated news item listed under "Hírek".
_Avoid_: News, article, hír

**Page**:
A standalone, undated content page edited in the block editor (e.g. "Rólunk", "Versenynaptár", "Kapcsolat").
_Avoid_: Static page, Sky page

**Club**:
A gymnastics club (egyesület) participating in the competition, shown with a logo and an optional website link.
_Avoid_: Egyesület, trainer, team

**Document**:
A downloadable file (typically PDF) with a title, belonging to exactly one Document Type.
_Avoid_: File, attachment, rule

**Document Type**:
A named grouping of Documents that gets its own listing on the site (e.g. regulations, competition announcements).
_Avoid_: Category, document category

### Competition

**Temporary Application**:
One of the two application types the organisers offer, submitted through its own external form. The distinction from a Permanent Application is defined by the organisers, not by the site.
_Avoid_: apply_temp, ideiglenes jelentkezés

**Permanent Application**:
One of the two application types the organisers offer, submitted through its own external form. The distinction from a Temporary Application is defined by the organisers, not by the site.
_Avoid_: apply_perma, állandó jelentkezés

### Administration

**Studio**:
The legacy name of the admin area; its old URL now leads to the WordPress admin.
_Avoid_: Panel, backend
